Client project

CC Medios Platform

Web platform for CC Medios (outdoor advertising) that unifies clients, billboards, orders, collections, payments and invoicing, with per-operation margin and due-date alerts.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • Vercel

Project facts

Type
Client project
Role
Process discovery, design and development
Status
Ongoing (since Aug 2026)
Stack
React, TypeScript, Supabase, Vercel

What I did

  • Mapped processes with the client.
  • Designed the data model, interface and role-based permissions.
  • Built and deployed with React, TypeScript, Supabase and Vercel, with AI assistance (Claude Code).
  • Worked in phased deliveries with demos.
  • Includes automatic reading of ARCA PDF invoices, automatically linked to their sales order.
  • Modules: Administration (clients, tasks, calendar, users and dashboard), Commercial (billboards, national map, own map and orders with sales lines, purchase lines and margin) and Financial (balances, collections and payments, payroll, office expenses, taxes and monthly consolidation).
